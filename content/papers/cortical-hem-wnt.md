## About

The cortical hem is a signalling centre in the embryonic forebrain that organises the development of the hippocampus. Wnt signalling genes are among its key signals. This project investigated how changing the expression of Wnt signalling genes affects the development of the cortical hem in the mouse.

## Methods

- **Gene overexpression and knockdown:** Wnt signalling genes were overexpressed or knocked down in the developing mouse brain, and the effects on the cortical hem were examined.
- **Immunohistochemistry (IHC):** used to examine the developing tissue and identify critical genes.
- **Bioinformatics and transcriptomics:** comparative analysis of transcriptomic datasets from the developing mouse brain, to identify differentially expressed genes.
