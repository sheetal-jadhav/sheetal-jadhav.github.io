Hi there! Welcome to my website.

My name is Sheetal Jadhav. I am an electronics engineer pursuing research in computational neuroscience.

I am currently transitioning into a PhD in Tristan Stöber's lab at the University of Göttingen, where I will work on building brain-inspired AGI models. My research lies at the intersection of neural dynamics, sequence learning, and biophysical modeling, with a focus on spiking neural networks, STDP-based learning rules, and hippocampal cognitive maps.

Previously, I worked in a songbird lab, where I conducted neuroelectrophysiological studies on the songbird brain to understand language syntax. My research so far has been highly interdisciplinary, spanning theoretical, neurobiological, and electrical labs.

Outside of research, I love science communication and writing. I run Brainy Tales, an educational platform on neuroscience, and write on Substack about the intersections of science and politics.
